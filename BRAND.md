# Brand guide: Hibiscus

The look takes its colour from the hibiscus flower, the zobo every Nigerian family knows. It should feel warm and welcoming, like somewhere you'd want to rest, with the hospital's calm close by but never clinical.

## Colour

Tokens live in `src/styles/index.css`. Components only use token names.

| Token           | Hex       | Use                                             |
| --------------- | --------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `page`          | `#FBF4F1` | Background, a soft blush white                  |
| `paper`         | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, the search bar, panels                   |
| `ink`           | `#2A0F1E` | Text, a deep aubergine                          |
| `muted`         | `#6B4A5A` | Supporting text                                 |
| `hibiscus`      | `#A3174B` | Primary buttons, links, selected rooms, icons   |
| `wine`          | `#5E0F2E` | Dark bands, the hero's fade, the footer         |
| `blush`         | `#F6D9E2` | Text on wine, soft fills                        |
| `mango`         | `#F6B93B` | The one warm highlight: badges, prices, accents |

Contrast: white on `hibiscus` 7.6, `hibiscus` on `page` 6.9, `muted` on `page` 7.0, `ink` on `mango` 10.1, `mango` on `wine` 7.5, `blush` on `wine` 10.1. All pass WCAG AA.

## Type

Self-hosted from Fontshare (free for commercial use):

- **Clash Display** for headlines, room names and prices: confident and modern.
- **Satoshi** for the interface and body text.
- Nothing smaller than 12px.

## Patterns

- **Hero:** a full-screen photo fading into wine, a huge headline and the search bar on top.
- **Search bar:** one white bar with reason, dates and guests in soft cells, and a hibiscus button.
- **House cards:** a photo with the area and a mango "from" price on it.
- **Rooms** are named the way reception names them (Single 1, Deluxe 2, VIP; Guest House 7 names every room) and shown by apartment or building.
- **Distance** is always stated as walking or driving minutes to the centre.
